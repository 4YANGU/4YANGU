StoYangu — online stores for Kenyan social-media sellers

StoYangu builds beautiful online storefronts for sellers across Kenya — clothes, jerseys, hoodies, perfumes, watches, shoes and other physical products. A seller shares one store link in TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp marketing; customers browse live products and place complete orders through WhatsApp.

How StoYangu works

Sellers apply with only a name and phone number. StoYangu builds and hosts the store on an address like storename.stoyangu.com, the owner manages products from a simple phone dashboard, and every evening at 7:30 PM the owner receives a daily update covering visitors, WhatsApp orders and champion products.

How much does StoYangu cost?

A professional store build is worth KES 15,000 — StoYangu waives it completely in exchange for a short 1-minute video about your business. The first 30 days are free, then store hosting and maintenance is KES 999 per month.
