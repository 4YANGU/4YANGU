WOYOYO-012 ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
=============================

1. Sign in with a real store-owner account on a phone.
2. Tap +. Choose a video or Skip. Add seven photos.
3. Confirm the media wraps downward: 3, 3, 2 with a video, or 3, 3, 1 without.
   No part of the dialog should extend beyond the phone screen.
4. Remove the first and last photos. Re-add a photo. Confirm previews stay visible.
5. Continue. Type and change the product name and price. Caption updates while
   typing without clicking a refresh/generate button.
6. Enable colours/sizes, choose some, then disable them. Caption matches only
   enabled details. Add a personal note, then change the price again. Note stays.
7. Save a draft. Open Inbox -> Saved drafts. Edit the caption and save. Reload.
   Confirm the change remains. Delete the draft and confirm it disappears.
8. With configured Repliz test credentials, connect each platform. Approve.
   Confirm the original store remains open and the account appears.
9. For Facebook/YouTube, approve and reload before selecting. Return to Inbox.
   Confirm the Page/channel picker returns, then select an account.
10. Repeat with a pop-up, with blocked pop-ups in a full browser tab, and on
    iOS Safari / Android Chrome / an installed PWA. OS-level behavior needs
    a real-device check and was not validated by the desktop browser tests.
11. Cancel permission. Confirm a readable error; no account becomes connected.
12. Disconnect and reconnect. Check the account belongs only to the right store.
13. Make a test post. Repliz accepts a scheduled post; verify delivery in the
    actual platform rather than assuming the API acceptance means published.
14. Sign out. The dashboard should require sign-in again.
15. On the production branch, confirm the login page has NO password-free
    buttons. This archive intentionally excludes all test-access code.

Automated local tests cover persistent auth and data flows, media wrapping,
caption updates, draft storage, and OAuth state recovery/security. Live Repliz
consent, real delivery, push notifications and device camera permissions require
appropriate external credentials and/or a real device.
